A Letter to You

Dear Jyo,

More than a decade. That is how long we have had you in our lives — and honestly, it still does not feel long enough. From the very beginning, you have been that friend who can walk into a room and instantly make it ten times better with your humor, your energy, and that voice of yours that can make anyone stop and listen.

You are the guy who turns every hangout into a concert, every silence into a joke, and every ordinary day into a memory we still laugh about years later. The kind of friend people are lucky to find once in a lifetime — and we got you for over ten years and counting.

We know you are far away now, and we do not get to see you nearly as much as we would like. But distance has never changed a thing between us. Every phone call picks up right where we left off. Every reunion feels like no time has passed at all. That is the kind of bond we have — unbreakable, no matter how many miles sit between us.

So on your special day, just know this: we miss you, we are ridiculously proud of you, and we cannot wait for the next time we are all together again — with you singing, making us laugh until our stomachs hurt, and reminding us why you are the best friend anyone could ever ask for.

Happy Birthday, you absolute legend!

Sending You
